Pocket Circuit

An arcade racer for iPhone: thirty circuits around the world, eight championship cups, a circuit builder, ghosts to chase and online races.

Support

Found a bug, have an idea, or need help? Email zalandan@gmail.com. Say which iPhone you have and what happened, and it'll be looked at.

Quick answers

How do I learn to drive? Settings → Driving School, or How to Play for the controls.

Someone has an offensive name. Tap their name in the room or on the rankings: report or block them.

How do I delete my online data? Settings → Delete online data. See the privacy policy.
