Pocket Circuit

Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Pocket Circuit is a racing game for iPhone, made by Daniel Bruinsma. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never asks for your real name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, garage, circuits you build, championship progress and best-lap replays ("ghosts") are saved on your phone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

What goes online, and why

Online features (world lap times, rankings, online races and the players-online count) run on Epic Online Services, a service of Epic Games. To use them the game signs in with an anonymous ID made for your phone. You don't make an account and no Epic account is needed. Online, the game stores:

  • the driver name you choose (or a random "Driver" name);
  • your best lap times on the built-in circuits, and your online rating and number of ranked races;
  • a last-seen time, updated every two minutes while the game is open, used only to count how many players are online;
  • while you're in an online race, your car, its position and your race result, sent to the other players in that race. Like any online game, the phones in a race connect through Epic's network, which sees their IP addresses;
  • reports you send about another player's name or behaviour (with the name they used), so they can be reviewed.

The game sends your phone's model name when it first creates its anonymous ID. Epic processes this data for the game under its own privacy policy: epicgames.com/site/privacypolicy.

None of this is sold, shared with advertisers or used to track you across apps or websites.

Other players

Driver names are filtered, and offensive ones are hidden. Tap any player's name in a room or on the rankings to report or block them. Reports are reviewed by the developer, who can remove names and players.

Deleting your data

In the game, Settings → Delete online data removes your name from the leaderboards, resets your online rating and makes the game forget its anonymous ID. To have your remaining lap times and rating removed from the leaderboards as well, email zalandan@gmail.com with your driver name. It's done within 30 days.

Children

Pocket Circuit doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children. It asks for none, and the only free text another player can see is a driver name, which is filtered.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: zalandan@gmail.com.
